# Trading Firms Overview

A comprehensive overview of the major proprietary trading firms, market makers, and quantitative hedge funds.

---

## Market Makers & HFT Firms

### Citadel Securities
- **Founded:** 2002 | **HQ:** Miami, FL | **Founder:** Ken Griffin
- **Employees:** ~1,700
- Largest designated market maker on the NYSE
- Handles an estimated **25% of all US equity volume**
- Operates across 50+ markets with real-time analytics, pricing, and risk management

### Jane Street
- **Founded:** ~2000 | **HQ:** New York City
- Global leader in ETF market making and liquidity
- Trades equities, bonds, options, and ETFs across 200+ electronic exchanges
- Reported **$10.1 billion in net trading revenue** in Q2 2025 (record)
- Highly regarded for quant research, technology, and trader development

### Virtu Financial
- **Founded:** 2008 | **HQ:** New York City | **CEO:** Doug Cifu
- Public company (NASDAQ: VIRT)
- Operates across **235+ venues in 36 countries**
- Designated market maker on NYSE and NYSE Amex
- Specializes in multi-asset trading: equities, fixed income, FX, commodities

### Optiver
- **Founded:** 1986 | **HQ:** Amsterdam, Netherlands
- ~1,000 employees globally
- One of the original electronic market makers in Europe
- Trades options, equities, ETFs, bonds, and FX across global exchanges

### IMC Trading
- **Founded:** 1989 | **HQ:** Amsterdam, Netherlands (US office in Chicago)
- Top-3 liquidity provider in listed options by volume
- Lead market maker for 150+ US ETFs
- Active in equities, fixed income, and derivatives globally

### Flow Traders
- **Founded:** 2004 | **HQ:** Amsterdam, Netherlands
- Europe's largest ETF market maker
- Provides liquidity for **13,000+ ETP listings** across North America, Europe, and Asia Pacific

### XTX Markets
- **Founded:** 2015 | **HQ:** London, UK
- ~100 employees; highly capital-efficient
- Systematic, data-driven market making in FX, equities, and fixed income
- Holds **~7% market share** in global FX (3rd largest non-bank FX market maker)
- **11%+ market share** in European equities

## Prop Trading Firms (Chicago-Style)

### Jump Trading
- **Founded:** 1999 | **HQ:** Chicago, IL | **Founders:** Paul Gurinas & Bill DiSomma
- Transitioned from floor trading to fully electronic HFT
- Active in futures, options, equities, and **crypto**
- Known for ultra-low-latency infrastructure and aggressive technology investment

### DRW
- **Founded:** 1992 | **HQ:** Chicago, IL | **Founder:** Don Wilson
- Market maker with roots in Eurodollar futures
- Active in fixed income, energy, FX, and digital assets
- Also operates **Cumberland**, a major crypto market maker

### Tower Research Capital
- **Founded:** 1998 | **HQ:** New York City | **Founder:** Mark Gorton
- "Siloed" team structure: independent teams share infrastructure but trade separate strategies
- Global footprint in equities, futures, options, and FX

### Akuna Capital
- **Founded:** 2011 | **HQ:** Chicago, IL
- Options market maker integrating quant research, AI, and algorithmic trading
- Known as a top destination for early-career quant traders and developers

## Quantitative Hedge Funds

### Renaissance Technologies
- **Founded:** 1982 | **HQ:** East Setauket, NY | **Founder:** Jim Simons
- Arguably the **most successful quant fund in history**
- The **Medallion Fund** is employee-only and has generated extraordinary returns for decades
- ~90 employees hold PhDs (math, physics, computer science, etc.)
- Operates by invitation only

### D.E. Shaw
- **Founded:** 1988 | **HQ:** New York City | **Founder:** David E. Shaw
- Pioneer in computational finance; Forbes called Shaw the "King Quant"
- Applies AI-driven strategies and exploits market inefficiencies
- Multi-asset: equities, fixed income, currencies, commodities, and private equity

### Two Sigma
- **Founded:** 2001 | **HQ:** New York City | **Founders:** John Overdeck & David Siegel
- Manages **$50B+ in AUM** (hedge fund)
- Uses data science, AI, and distributed computing
- Also operates **Two Sigma Securities**, a market maker trading 300M+ shares/day in the US

### Millennium Management
- **Founded:** 1989 | **HQ:** New York City | **Founder:** Israel Englander
- Multi-manager hedge fund: independent trading teams operate within shared infrastructure
- Manages **$60B+ in AUM**
- Quant-heavy with systematic and discretionary strategies

### Point72 Asset Management
- **Founded:** 2014 (successor to SAC Capital) | **HQ:** Stamford, CT | **Founder:** Steve Cohen
- Manages **~$41.5B in AUM** (as of late 2025)
- 3,000+ employees across offices in US, Europe, and Asia
- Multi-strategy: long/short equity, macro, systematic (via **Cubist** division), and venture capital

### Susquehanna International Group (SIG)
- **Founded:** 1987 | **HQ:** Bala Cynwyd, PA | **Founder:** Jeff Yass
- 3,500+ employees in 17+ offices worldwide
- Started as options market maker; now covers equities, fixed income, energy, commodities, and private equity
- Applies **game theory** and machine learning to options trading
- Major investor in technology companies (notably TikTok parent ByteDance)

## Key Distinctions

- **Market Makers** earn the bid-ask spread by continuously quoting buy and sell prices and providing liquidity.
- **HFT Firms** use ultra-low-latency infrastructure to exploit short-lived pricing inefficiencies.
- **Prop Trading Firms** trade the firm's own capital with no external clients.
- **Quant Hedge Funds** manage external investor capital using systematic/algorithmic strategies.
- **Multi-Manager Funds** (Millennium, Point72) run many independent internal teams under one umbrella.
